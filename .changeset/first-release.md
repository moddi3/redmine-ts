First release. A typed, zero-dependency client for the Redmine REST API, for Redmine 6.1 and 7.0. Types and operations are generated from the community Redmine OpenAPI spec. It covers every resource in the spec, filters lists with typed operators, pages with `for await`, uploads and downloads attachments, impersonates users, and throws one `RedmineError` type with a `kind` for each failure.
